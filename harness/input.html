Gestures

Stick is anchored in the marked corner and lit at rest: a thumb inside the capture radius grabs it, one further out keeps its own origin while the ring stays put. Camera is everywhere else — drag to look, two fingers pinch/twist.
Punch tap = light, hold = charge ring, swipe up = uppercut. Double tap = jump. Two-finger tap = lock on. Dash toggles.
